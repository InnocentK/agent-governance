# Multi-task checklist (standing directive)

When the user's request contains **more than two distinct tasks** — separately
actionable asks, not sub-steps of one task — (or you decompose it into more than
two), create and maintain a checklist to track them. Use your
native to-do / task-list mechanism if you have one; otherwise keep an explicit
written checklist in your responses. Keep it updated as items complete, and close
with a short summary of what was done and what remains outstanding, so both you and
the user can reconstruct afterward what happened.

This is about traceability across multi-part requests; it does not apply to a
single task that merely has several internal steps.
